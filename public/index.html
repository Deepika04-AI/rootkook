Record a recipe with an elder

Sit with them while they cook. RootKook asks the questions and writes it down.

Let the AI interview your elder one question at a time, out loud and in their own language, or paste what they already told you (a voice-note transcript, a WhatsApp message, a photo of their handwritten card). You get a recipe card that keeps their words, their measures, and the story behind the dish.

What you get

A card your family can cook from in 20 years

  1. Their words, kept. The story stays in their voice. "A fistful" stays a fistful, with an approximate measure beside it.
  2. Gaps flagged. RootKook lists what's still unclear so you know what to ask at the next family dinner.
  3. Cook-along mode. Big-text steps, timers, and a safe job for each child, ages 5 to 16.
  4. Ask the recipe. "No curry leaves in Ohio, what now?" Answers stay true to how they cook it.
